01 / About

FloodSafe PH is an ongoing civic technology project focused on disaster risk reduction in low-lying Philippine urban centers and river basins. It integrates live sensor telemetry with community-accessible maps to deliver rapid flood warnings.

02 / The Problem

During typhoons and monsoon seasons, water levels in urban waterways can rise rapidly within minutes:

  • Fragmented information: Residents must check disparate social media posts and manual siren broadcasts to know current river levels.
  • Delayed evacuation decisions: Without visible trendlines (e.g. rising at 0.5m / 15 mins), families hesitate until roads are already submerged.
  • Unknown safe paths: Evacuees frequently attempt routes that pass through known choke points and impassable flooding.

03 / The Solution

FloodSafe PH provides a single, mobile-optimized dashboard designed to function cleanly under degraded network conditions:

  • Live Sensor Telemetry: Real-time gauge levels classified under 3 alarm tiers (Alert, Warning, Evacuation).
  • Lightweight Geospatial Mapping: Interactive map rendering evacuation centers, high-ground shelters, and flooded street pins.
  • Trend Forecasting: Visual sparklines showing 1-hour and 3-hour water velocity trajectories.
  • SMS & Push Notification Hub: Automated alerts sent when upstream sensors cross warning thresholds.

06 / Challenges

1. Resilient Offline Map Caching

Mobile connectivity often drops during extreme weather events. Implementing Service Worker tile caching for the user's localized 5km radius ensures maps remain readable even when cell towers lose power.

2. Sensor Data Smoothing & Anomaly Rejection

Ultrasonic water sensors can report transient spikes due to floating debris or wave chop. Implemented a rolling moving average filter to reject false threshold triggers.

07 / What I Learned

Developing FloodSafe PH showed me the direct social impact of software engineering. I gained practical knowledge in geospatial visualization, PWA caching strategies, and designing resilient systems for real-world constraints.
