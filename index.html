Background

About Me

I'm Adam Nuevo, a 4th-year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) student based in Quezon City, Philippines.

My journey into software development isn't based on corporate claims—most of what I understand has come directly from writing code, breaking things, reading documentation, and working through errors until a system works reliably.

What I enjoy: I find satisfaction in designing clean, readable user interfaces and connecting them to well-structured databases and backends. I prefer code that is straightforward, maintainable, and solves real day-to-day problems over unnecessary complexity.

How I learn: I learn best by building complete projects from start to finish. When I want to understand a technology (whether it's Next.js, Laravel, or relational SQL indexing), I build an actual application around it.

Current Goal: I am actively seeking an OJT / Internship opportunity where I can contribute to real-world codebases, learn alongside experienced engineering teams, and continue growing into a solid software developer.
